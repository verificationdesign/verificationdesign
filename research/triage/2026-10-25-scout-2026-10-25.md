## Candidate: Evaluate the Stack, Not the Layer: Do Deterministic and LLM Gates for Agent Actions Fail Independently?

Initial label: narrows
Confidence: medium

### Abstract Paraphrase

The study measures joint misses in stacks of deterministic rules and model judges on 1,119 labeled tool actions.
